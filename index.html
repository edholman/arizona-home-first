Works Alongside Down Payment Assistance

Arizona has strong down payment assistance programs, like HOME Plus statewide and Home in Five in Maricopa County. Those programs help with your down payment. Our credits help with your closing costs. Used together, they can greatly reduce the cash you need to buy your first home.

Assistance programs are run by their own agencies and have their own income limits and rules. Our credits are separate from and not part of any government program.

About Arizona Home First

Arizona's housing market has become hard for first time buyers. Many people with good credit and steady income are still held back by the cash needed at closing. Arizona Home First was created to change that by connecting buyers with licensed professionals who put part of their own compensation back into the buyer's pocket.

Our network includes licensed Arizona real estate agents and a licensed mortgage lender. Every credit is disclosed in writing and reflected on your closing statement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Arizona Home First a government program?

No. Arizona Home First is a private homebuyer program. The credits come from participating licensed real estate agents and a licensed mortgage lender, not from a government agency.

How much can I get in credits?

Qualified buyers may receive up to $10,000 or more toward closing costs, depending on the home price and loan. Credits equal about 1% of the loan amount from the lender plus 1% of the purchase price from a partner real estate agent. For example, a $510,000 purchase with 3.5% down works out to about $10,000, and a $400,000 purchase to about $7,860. Use the calculator above for your estimate.

Do I have to use your lender?

No. You are always free to choose any lender. The lender credit applies when you finance with our program lender, and the agent credit applies when you buy with a participating partner agent.

Can the credits be used for my down payment?

Generally no. Lender and agent credits are usually applied to closing costs, prepaid items, or a rate buydown, within your loan program's limits. They can be combined with down payment assistance programs that cover the down payment.

What if the same person is my agent and my loan officer?

If a licensed professional acts as both your real estate agent and your loan officer, you will receive a written dual capacity disclosure and agreement to review and sign before moving forward. You can always choose a different agent or lender.

What does it cost to check my eligibility?

Nothing. Checking your eligibility is free and does not affect your credit score.
